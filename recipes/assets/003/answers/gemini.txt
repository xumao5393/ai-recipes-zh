### 3. Product Description

Bring convenience to your outdoor adventures with the Collapsible Silicone Travel Water Bottle. This portable and reusable cup is designed for active lifestyles, making it a great companion for camping, traveling, hiking, and sports.

The bottle features a food-grade silicone cup body, a PP lid, and an ABS plastic carabiner clip. When expanded, it holds 17 oz (500 ml) and measures 5.9 inches in height with a 2.7-inch top diameter. When you are ready to pack up, it collapses down to just 1.8 inches in height and weighs only 4.2 oz (120 g), saving valuable space in your bag.

**Temperature Ratings:**

* **Cup Body (Silicone):** -40°F to 446°F (-40°C to 230°C)
* **Lid (PP):** Up to 212°F (100°C)

**Care and Safety Notes:**

* Dishwasher safe (top rack only).
* Do not place in the microwave.
* **Important Note:** The lid is a snap-on design and is **not leak-proof**. The bottle will leak if inverted or laid horizontally.

Each package includes 1 cup and 1 carabiner clip packaged in a color box. Available colors include gray, blue, and pink.
