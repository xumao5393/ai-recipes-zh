## 3. 产品描述（Description）

This collapsible silicone water cup is designed for camping, hiking, travel and everyday outdoor use. When open, it holds **16.9 fl oz (500 ml)** and stands **5.9 in (15 cm)** tall. When folded, it compresses to **1.8 in (4.5 cm)**, so it takes up little space in a bag or pack. The **2.8 in (7 cm)** wide mouth makes it easy to fill and drink from.

The cup body is made of food-grade silicone. The lid is PP plastic and the hanging clip is ABS plastic. The silicone body is rated from **-40°F to 446°F (-40°C to 230°C)**, while the PP lid is rated up to **212°F (100°C)**. Please keep the lid's temperature limit in mind when filling the cup.

For cleaning, place the cup on the **top rack of the dishwasher only**. Do not use the microwave to heat it. The lid snaps onto the cup but does not seal, so keep the cup upright during carrying.

The package includes **1 cup, 1 hanging clip**, packed in a colored box. Available in **gray, blue and pink**.
