#### How are Max and Ultra different from Pro?
Max and Ultra are built for people who work with Claude throughout the day and want longer, uninterrupted sessions. You get everything in Pro, plus more usage. Max gives you 5x the usage of Pro, every 5 hours and every week. Ultra gives you 10x the weekly usage of Pro, with no 5-hour limit for verified accounts. Both plans include Fable, our top model, for up to half your weekly limit, and a monthly API credit to build on the Claude Platform: $100 on Max, $200 on Ultra. Both plans are billed monthly. See the cards above for current pricing.
