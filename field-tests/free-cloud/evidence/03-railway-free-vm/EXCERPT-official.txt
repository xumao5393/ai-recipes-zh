关键句摘录：
- Get a VM for free. One command gets you a Linux VM in about 1.4 seconds, with coding agents preinstalled and a live preview URL.
- $ ssh railway.new
- Welcome to your Railway VM. You have 60 min to try it out.
- CPU 2 vCPU / Memory 2 GB / Ready in ~1.4 s / Build time 60 min / Claim time 24 h
- No dashboard, no config, no card.
- Bring any SSH key, that's your login.
- Do I need a Railway account? No. Railway identifies you by your SSH key.
- What happens after 60 minutes? The build window closes. You then have 24 hours to claim the box... Unclaimed boxes are deleted along with their files.
- What's installed? The Railway Agent (run agent), Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Cursor CLI, Grok, and pi, plus Node, Python, gh, mise, the Railway CLI, and Chromium with Playwright.
- Is it really free? Yes. While a box is unclaimed, nobody pays for its compute, and you never enter a card.
- When demand is high... "Anonymous trials are temporarily disabled..."
